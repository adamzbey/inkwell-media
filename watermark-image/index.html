Add a text or image watermark

Free, private, in-browser image watermarking — nothing leaves your device. Add custom text or your logo, set the position, size, colour and opacity, and watermark one photo or a whole batch at once.

How watermarking works here

Add text or your own logo over an image, then set its position, size, and opacity. Your browser composites the watermark onto a new copy of the picture and hands it back to download — all locally, with nothing uploaded and your original left untouched.

When to add a watermark

  • Protect your work: mark photos before sharing them publicly.
  • Brand your images: add a logo or name to product shots and social posts.
  • Label proofs: stamp “DRAFT” or “PROOF” on previews you send for review.
  • Claim attribution: add a credit line to portfolio or press images.

Tips for an effective watermark

A watermark works best when it's semi-transparent — visible enough to deter reuse, but light enough not to ruin the image. Placing it across an important part of the picture (not just tucked in a corner) makes it harder to crop out. Keep the same style across your images for consistent branding. A text watermark is quickest for a name or copyright line, while an image watermark lets you place your actual logo — save the result as a PNG if the mark needs a transparent edge.

Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Watermarking happens entirely in your browser, so your images never leave your device.

Can I use my own logo as the watermark?

Yes. Add text, or overlay your own logo image.

Can a watermark be removed?

It deters casual copying, but no visible watermark is completely tamper-proof. Placing it over key areas at moderate opacity makes removal harder.

Can I watermark several images at once?

Yes. Apply the same watermark across a batch and download them together.

Is there a size limit?

The maximum is 150 MB per image; under about 40 MB is recommended. High-resolution images may exceed available browser memory even below the maximum.
