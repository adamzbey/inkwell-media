Private browser tool

Make a Wi-Fi QR code

Type your network name and password and get a QR code guests can scan to join, on iPhone and Android, without typing anything. Print it for the fridge, a guest room, an Airbnb or a front desk. It’s made in your browser, so your password isn’t sent anywhere.

Ideas

  • Guest rooms and rentals frame it next to the house rules.
  • Offices and waiting rooms for the guest network.
  • Cafés and shops on the counter or the menu.
  • Events on a sign by the door.

Where to find your network details

The network name and password are usually on a sticker on your router. Pick WPA unless your router is very old. If you change the password later, make a new code.

Frequently asked questions

Do iPhones and Android phones both scan it?

Yes. Open the camera, point it at the code and tap the “Join network” prompt.

Is my password sent anywhere?

No. The code is made in your browser and your password never leaves your device.

Does the code expire?

No. It keeps working until you change your Wi-Fi name or password.

Can I add our logo?

Yes. Add it in the options, then test the code with your phone before printing.
