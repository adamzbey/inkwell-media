Join videos into one MP4

Pick your clips, put them in order and download one MP4. Clips of different sizes are fitted to the first one. Free and private: the joining happens on your device.

How it works

Each clip is resized to match the first, set to the same frame rate, and played one after another into a single MP4 that works everywhere. Clips without sound get silence, so the sound stays in sync.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

How many clips can I join?

As many as your browser’s memory allows. A few short phone clips are fine anywhere; for long or 4K videos, use a computer.

Will the quality drop?

Clips are re-encoded at a high quality setting, so the change is hard to see.
