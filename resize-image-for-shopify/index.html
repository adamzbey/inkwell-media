Why the exact size matters

Shopify themes show product images in a square grid. When photos are different shapes, the grid looks uneven. Making them all the same square, with a white border if you don’t want anything cropped, keeps the store tidy.

Your photos stay on your device

Most resizers upload your photo to their server first. This one does the work in your browser, so nothing is sent anywhere. Resizing also leaves out the hidden location and camera details that phones save inside photos.

Frequently asked questions

What size should Shopify product images be?

Shopify recommends 2048 × 2048 pixels for square product images.

Can I keep the whole product in the photo?

Yes. Choose “Fit inside and add a border” and pick white (or any colour) for the background.

Can I do all my product photos at once?

Yes. Select them all and download one ZIP.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser.
