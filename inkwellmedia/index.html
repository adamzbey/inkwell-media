What you can do with Inkwell Media

Inkwell Media is a small collection of practical image, video, and audio tools that run entirely on your device. No accounts, no watermarks, and no uploads — every file is processed in your browser and never sent to a server.

  • Sherpa: after the check-up, pick where the file is going (Instagram, Etsy, email, your website, printing and more) and Sherpa says what to change and opens the right tool. Optional Sherpa AI uses your own free AI key and sends only file details, never the file.
  • Check-up: drop photos or videos to see hidden location and camera details, formats that won’t open, and files too big to send, then fix them in one go.
  • Image tools: remove location and camera data, compress, convert between formats, resize to exact sizes for popular sites, crop to a shape, rotate and flip, add a watermark, remove backgrounds, and turn HEIC photos into JPG. Most work on a whole batch at once.
  • Video tools: compress clips (including to fit Discord or email), convert to MP4, make a sharp GIF, trim to the exact frame, and remove the location from phone videos.
  • Audio tools: extract the audio track from a video file.

Private by design — nothing leaves your device

Most tools that edit media upload your file to a server first. Inkwell Media doesn't. Everything happens locally in your browser using your own device, so sensitive photos, client footage, and personal recordings stay with you. Because the work is local, there is nothing to leak and nothing to delete afterward — and the tools keep working even on a slow or offline connection once loaded.

Why choose in-browser tools?

Uploading a file to a website means waiting for the upload, trusting a company with your data, and hoping the file is deleted afterward. Running the same job locally skips all of that: there is no upload wait even for large files, no server storing your media, no account to create, and no per-file size caps set by someone else. Once a tool has loaded it keeps working even if your connection drops. The trade-off is that heavy jobs use your own device, which is why the image tools feel instant everywhere while the video tools are happiest on a computer.

How can these tools be free and private?

Traditional online tools upload your file to a company's server, process it there, and send it back — which is why they tend to need accounts, usage limits, and your data. Inkwell Media works the opposite way. Modern browsers can now run the same kind of processing that used to require installed software, using technology like WebAssembly for video and the built-in canvas for images. The work happens on your own computer or phone, using power you already have.

Because there's no server doing the heavy lifting, there's nothing to meter and nothing to pay for — so the tools stay free, with no sign-up. It also means your files never travel across the internet, and once a tool has loaded, most keep working even if your connection drops. Privacy isn't a feature added on afterward here; it's simply how the tools are built.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Every tool processes your file directly in your browser. Your images, video, and audio never leave your device.

Do I need to create an account?

No account, no sign-up, and no cost. Open a tool, drop in a file, and go.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After your first visit the pages and image tools work without a connection. Video tools, background removal, face blurring and auto captions work offline after you’ve used them once, because their engines and models download the first time.

Does it work on my phone?

The image tools work well on phones. The video and audio tools run best on a computer, since they use more of your device's processing power.

Open-source software we use

Inkwell Media runs on open-source software, unmodified: ffmpeg.wasm (FFmpeg, with x264; GPL/LGPL), IMG.LY background-removal (AGPL-3.0), heic-to with libheif (LGPL-3.0) fflate (MIT), face-api with TensorFlow.js (MIT, Apache-2.0) and Transformers.js (Apache-2.0) running OpenAI’s Whisper speech model (MIT). Source code for each is available at those links. Inkwell Media’s own code is published under the AGPL-3.0 licence on GitHub.
