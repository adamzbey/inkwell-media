Private browser tool

Auto captions for TikTok and Reels

Most people watch short videos with the sound off. Drop your clip and get captions written for you and drawn into the picture, on a dark box in the lower middle, clear of the app’s buttons. No watermark, and the video never leaves your device.

How it works

A speech model runs in your browser (a one-time 40 MB download), writes the captions with timings, and draws them into your video. Download the SRT too if you want to fix a word and burn it in again with Add subtitles to video.

Tips

Speak clearly and keep music low for the best captions. Read them through before posting: names and slang can come out wrong.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

Is there a watermark?

No.

Which languages work?

English works best; Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch and many others work too.
