Resize a video for Reels, TikTok and Shorts

Got a landscape video and need it vertical? Make it 9:16 without cutting anything off: the whole picture sits in the middle on a soft blurred copy of itself, the look you see all over Reels and TikTok. Or crop to fill, or add plain bars. Also 4:5 for the feed and square.

Which shape for where

  • 9:16 (1080 × 1920): Instagram Reels and Stories, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels.
  • 4:5 (1080 × 1350): Instagram and Facebook feed posts; takes the most room in the feed.
  • 1:1 (1080 × 1080): square posts that work everywhere.
  • 16:9 (1920 × 1080): turn a vertical phone video into a landscape one for YouTube.

Blurred background, crop or bars?

A blurred background keeps everything in the shot and looks finished. Crop to fill fills the screen but trims the sides, so it suits clips where the action is in the middle. Plain bars are the simplest and keep the picture exactly as it was.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

Will the sound be kept?

Yes. The sound is copied into the new MP4.

How long can the video be?

Short clips work best in the browser. A minute or two at Full HD is fine on a laptop; untick Full HD for a faster 720 version.
