Compress a video for Discord

Get a clip under 20 MB, Discord’s upload limit on the free plan, without signing up for anything. We work out the quality from the clip’s length so it lands just under the limit.

How it fits under 20 MB

File size depends on length and quality. The tool reads how long your clip is, works out the highest quality that fits in 20 MB, and lowers the resolution for longer clips so they still look clean. If the first try comes out a little big, it tries again a step smaller.

Tips for the best result

  • Trim first: the shorter the clip, the better it looks at 20 MB. Use Trim video to keep just the moment.
  • Short clips look best: under a minute stays sharp; several minutes will look soft.
  • Use a computer for long videos: everything runs on your device, and phones are slower.

Frequently asked questions

What is Discord’s upload limit?

Discord raised its free upload limit to 20 MB in August 2026. Nitro plans allow more.

Is my video uploaded?

No. Compression runs in your browser and your video never leaves your device.

How long can the video be?

Short clips work best. If a video is too long to look decent at 20 MB, we’ll say so and suggest trimming it first.

What format do I get?

An MP4 (H.264), which plays in Discord and almost everywhere else.
