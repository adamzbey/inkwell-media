Convert MP4 to MP3

Save the sound from a video as a high-quality MP3: a lecture, a meeting recording, a song you played or a voice note. Works with MP4, MOV, WebM and more. Free, and the video never leaves your device.

How it works

The audio track is taken out of the video and saved as an MP3 that plays on any phone, computer or car. The picture is dropped, so the file is much smaller.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

Can I convert music videos from YouTube?

Only use videos you made or have the right to use. This tool works on files on your own device; it doesn’t download from websites.

Which formats work?

MP4, MOV, M4V, WebM, MKV and AVI.
