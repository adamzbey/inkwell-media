Blur faces and license plates before you post

Free and private: faces are found for you on your own device, and you can drag across anything else you want hidden, like a license plate, a house number or a name tag. Choose blur, pixelate or a solid block, then download.

How it works

A small face-finding model runs in your browser and marks every face it can see. Each one gets a box you can keep or tap away. Drag across the picture to add your own boxes for plates, screens, documents or anything else. The download is a new copy: your original photo isn’t changed, and the copy has its location and camera details removed too.

When to use it

  • Selling a car: hide the plate in listing photos.
  • Posting kids or crowds: blur faces of people who didn’t agree to be online.
  • Screenshots and documents: cover names, addresses and account numbers.
  • Neighbourhood posts: hide house numbers and street signs.

Blur, pixelate or block?

Strong blur looks natural and is fine for faces. For text such as plates, names or numbers, a solid block is the safest choice, because blurred or pixelated text can sometimes be guessed. Whatever you pick, check the preview before you share.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

Does it find every face?

It finds most faces that are facing the camera and reasonably large. Small, blurry or turned-away faces can be missed, so always check the preview and drag over any it skipped.

Can it find license plates by itself?

No. Plates are marked by dragging a box over them. It takes a second and works on any plate shape or country.

Can someone undo the blur?

Not from the file we give you: the original pixels are replaced. For text, use Solid block to be completely safe.

Does it remove location data too?

Yes. The new copy has no GPS location, camera or date details.
