Convert a GIF to MP4

GIFs are big and some sites won’t take them. An MP4 of the same animation is usually 5–20 times smaller and uploads anywhere a video does. Repeat short loops 3 or 5 times if you like. Free and private.

How it works

The GIF is turned into an H.264 MP4 in your browser, the format every phone, browser and social site plays. The size is kept (rounded to even numbers, which video needs).

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

Will it loop?

Videos play once unless the player loops them. Choose Repeat 3 or 5 times to make a short loop longer.

Does it have sound?

No. GIFs have no sound, so the MP4 is silent.
