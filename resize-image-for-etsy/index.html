Why the exact size matters

Etsy recommends listing photos at least 2000 pixels wide and tall, and suggests a square or landscape first photo so its thumbnails crop cleanly. Photos taken at home can also carry your home location, so it’s worth removing that before you list.

Your photos stay on your device

Most resizers upload your photo to their server first. This one does the work in your browser, so nothing is sent anywhere. Resizing also leaves out the hidden location and camera details that phones save inside photos.

Frequently asked questions

What size should Etsy listing photos be?

Etsy recommends at least 2000 pixels on each side. Square 2000 × 2000 is the simplest; 4:3 landscape (2667 × 2000) also works well for a first photo.

Can I resize a whole batch of product photos?

Yes. Select them all and download one ZIP.

My photos are smaller than 2000 pixels.

They’ll be enlarged, which can look soft. We’ll tell you when that happens so you can use a bigger original if you have one.

Do my photos show where I live?

They can. Phone photos often include GPS location. Use Remove location to clean them before listing.

Is anything uploaded?

No. It all happens in your browser.
