Private browser tool

Auto captions, made on your device

Drop a video or recording and get subtitles in a few minutes. Speech is turned into text by an AI model that runs in your browser, so the recording is never uploaded. Download SRT for YouTube, LinkedIn and Facebook, VTT for web players, a plain transcript, or an MP4 with captions viewers can switch on.

How it works

The first time, your browser downloads OpenAI’s open Whisper speech model (about 40 MB, or 80 MB for the more accurate setting) and keeps it for next time. The sound is taken out of your video, listened to in chunks, and turned into short caption lines with timings. Nothing is sent to a server.

Where to use the files

  • YouTube: Subtitles → Add language → Upload file → SRT.
  • LinkedIn and Facebook: upload the SRT when you post the video.
  • Premiere, DaVinci Resolve, CapCut: import the SRT as a caption track.
  • Your website: use the VTT with the HTML video player.

Check before you publish

Machine captions get most words right, but names, jargon and mumbled words can be wrong. Open the SRT in any text editor (it’s plain text) and fix anything before you upload.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

Which languages work?

English works best. Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch and many others work too; pick “Other” to let the model detect the language.

How long does it take?

On a laptop, roughly a minute of work for every few minutes of speech with the Fast setting. Phones are slower.

Can it burn the captions into the picture?

Yes. Choose “MP4 with captions always shown” and pick a caption style. Or choose the MP4 option for captions viewers can switch on with the CC button.
