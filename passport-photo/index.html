Make a passport or ID photo at home

Take a photo against a plain light wall, drop it in and pick your document. We find your face and line it up to the official head size; drag or zoom to fine-tune. Download a single photo for online applications or a 4×6 sheet to print.

Sizes included

  • U.S. passport and visa: 2 × 2 in, head 1–1⅜ in.
  • UK passport: 35 × 45 mm, head 29–34 mm.
  • Schengen and EU visas: 35 × 45 mm, head 32–36 mm.
  • Canada passport: 50 × 70 mm, head 31–36 mm.
  • Australia passport: 35 × 45 mm, head 32–36 mm.

Tips for a photo that’s accepted

Face the camera with a neutral expression and both eyes open. Take glasses off. Use even light with no shadows on your face or the wall behind you. Have someone else take it from about 1.2 m (4 ft) away at head height, rather than a selfie, which distorts the face.

Printing the sheet

The 4×6 in sheet is 300 dpi with several copies and thin cut lines. Order a standard 4×6 print at a pharmacy or photo counter, or print at home at actual size (turn off “fit to page”), then cut along the lines.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

Will my photo be accepted?

We get the size and head position right, but the rules also cover lighting, expression, background and glasses. Check your country’s official guidance; the final decision is the passport office’s.

Can it change my background to white?

Not automatically. Stand in front of a plain white or light grey wall for the best result.

What size is the digital file?

U.S.: 600 × 600 px. UK: 900 × 1200 px. Others at 600 dpi equivalents, within the usual online-application limits.
