Make a favicon and app icons from your logo

Drop your logo and download a ZIP with everything a website needs: favicon.ico, sharp PNG icons from 16 to 512 px, an SVG icon, the Apple touch icon, Android and “maskable” icons, a site.webmanifest and the exact lines to paste into your page.

What’s in the ZIP

  • favicon.ico with 16, 32 and 48 px inside, for every browser.
  • favicon.svg and favicon-32x32.png for modern browsers.
  • apple-touch-icon.png (180 px) for iPhone home screens.
  • icon-192.png, icon-512.png and a maskable icon for Android and installable apps.
  • site.webmanifest and paste-into-head.html.

Tips

Use a square logo or just the symbol part of it: tiny icons can’t show words. A transparent PNG or an SVG gives the sharpest result. For Shopify, Wix or Squarespace, upload icon-512.png in the site settings instead.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

My logo is wide. Will it be squashed?

No. It’s fitted inside the square with space around it, never stretched.

What is a maskable icon?

Android may crop app icons into circles or rounded squares. The maskable icon has extra space so your logo isn’t cut off.
