Add subtitles to a video (burned in)

Have an SRT or VTT file? Drop it in with your video and download an MP4 with the words drawn into the picture, so they show on Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp and anywhere captions can’t be switched on. Pick white on a dark box, an outline, or bold yellow.

How it works

Each caption is drawn as a sharp, transparent overlay and shown at its exact time, then the video is re-encoded as an MP4. The sound is kept. Your original files aren’t changed.

No subtitle file yet?

Use Auto captions: it listens to the video on your device and makes an SRT, or burns the captions in straight away. Download the SRT too, so you can fix a word in any text editor and burn it in again here.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

Which subtitle files work?

SRT and WebVTT (.vtt), the two common formats from YouTube, Premiere, CapCut, Descript and transcription services.

Can viewers turn them off?

No, that’s the point of burning them in. If you want captions viewers can switch on and off, upload the SRT alongside the video on YouTube, LinkedIn or Facebook instead.
