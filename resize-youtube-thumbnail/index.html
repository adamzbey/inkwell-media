Why the exact size matters

YouTube shows thumbnails everywhere from phones to TVs. A 16:9 image at 1280 × 720 looks crisp in all of them, and YouTube rejects thumbnails over 2 MB when you upload from a phone. This tool lowers the JPG quality a step at a time until the file fits.

Your photos stay on your device

Most resizers upload your photo to their server first. This one does the work in your browser, so nothing is sent anywhere. Resizing also leaves out the hidden location and camera details that phones save inside photos.

Frequently asked questions

What size is a YouTube thumbnail?

1280 × 720 pixels, a 16:9 shape, with a minimum width of 640 pixels.

What is the file size limit?

YouTube’s limit for thumbnails uploaded from a phone is 2 MB. We keep the file under that so it works wherever you upload from.

Should I use PNG or JPG?

Either works. JPG is the safer choice for staying under 2 MB; PNGs that are too big are saved as JPG automatically.

Is my image uploaded?

No. It’s resized in your browser and never leaves your device.
