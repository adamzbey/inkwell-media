Convert HEIC photos to JPG

Free, private, in-browser HEIC to JPG conversion — nothing leaves your device. Turn iPhone HEIC or HEIF photos into JPGs that open everywhere, one at a time or a batch, without sending them to a server. Works offline after the first use.

How HEIC to JPG conversion works here

HEIC is the high-efficiency photo format iPhones use by default, and many websites, apps, and Windows or Android devices can't open it. This tool decodes HEIC files in your browser and re-saves them as widely supported JPGs. It runs locally, so your photos are never uploaded, and a batch comes back as a single ZIP.

When to convert HEIC to JPG

  • Uploads that reject HEIC: get iPhone photos onto forms, portals, and sites that only take JPG.
  • Sharing across devices: send photos that open cleanly on Windows and Android.
  • Editing elsewhere: open your pictures in apps that don't read HEIC.
  • Batch a camera roll: convert a whole set of photos at once.

Why are my photos HEIC in the first place?

Apple switched to HEIC because it stores the same quality in roughly half the space of JPG — great on your iPhone, awkward everywhere else. Converting to JPG trades a little of that space savings for compatibility that works on practically any device or site.

Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. Conversion runs entirely in your browser, so your photos never leave your device.

Will I lose quality converting to JPG?

Only slightly. JPG re-compresses the image, but at normal quality the difference is hard to see.

Can I convert many photos at once?

Yes. Drop in a batch and download them together as a ZIP.

Can I stop my iPhone making HEIC files?

Yes — in Camera settings choose “Most Compatible” to shoot JPG going forward. This tool handles the HEIC photos you already have.

Is there a size limit?

Up to 100 MB per photo, as many photos as you like; under about 40 MB is recommended. High-resolution photos may exceed available browser memory even below the maximum.
