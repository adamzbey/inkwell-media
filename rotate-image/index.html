Rotate or flip an image instantly

Free, private, in-browser image rotation and flipping — nothing leaves your device. Turn images clockwise or counter-clockwise, rotate 180°, or mirror them, one at a time or a whole batch the same way.

How rotating and flipping works here

Turn an image in 90° steps or mirror it horizontally or vertically, and your browser re-renders the pixels in the new orientation, then gives you the file to download. It runs locally, so nothing is uploaded, and the change is baked into the image itself rather than stored as metadata that some apps ignore.

When to rotate or flip an image

  • Fix sideways photos: correct phone pictures that show up rotated.
  • Straighten scans: put a scanned document or photo the right way up.
  • Mirror an image: flip a picture for a design or layout.
  • Prep for upload: set the correct orientation before sending an image somewhere.

About phone photo orientation (EXIF)

Phones often store rotation as a piece of metadata called EXIF orientation rather than actually rotating the pixels. That's why a photo can look upright on your phone but sideways somewhere else. Re-saving it here writes the correct orientation into the pixels, so it displays the same way everywhere.

Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Rotating and flipping happen entirely in your browser, so your images never leave your device.

Does rotating reduce quality?

Rotating in 90° steps and flipping are lossless for PNG and re-encode JPGs only slightly — you won't see a meaningful difference.

Why did my photo look rotated elsewhere?

Some apps ignore the EXIF orientation tag phones use. Saving a corrected copy here fixes it everywhere.

Can I rotate a batch at once?

Yes. Process multiple images and download them together as a ZIP.

Is there a size limit?

The maximum is 150 MB per image; under about 40 MB is recommended. High-resolution images may exceed available browser memory even below the maximum.
