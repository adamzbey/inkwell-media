Make a before and after photo

Pick two photos and download one image with them side by side or stacked, labelled Before and After (or your own words). Great for renovations, detailing, cleaning, haircuts, gardens and photo edits.

Tips

Take both photos from the same spot and the same distance so the change is easy to see. Square or Instagram portrait sizes work best for social posts; “Fit the layout” keeps more of each photo.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

Can I change the labels?

Yes, type any words, or turn the labels off.

Are the photos stretched?

No. Each photo is trimmed evenly to fill its half.
