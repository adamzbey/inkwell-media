Turn a podcast clip into a video

Drop an audio clip and, if you like, your cover art. Pick the best 15 to 90 seconds, add a title, choose the colours and the shape, and get an MP4 with a sound wave that moves with the voice. Social apps play video, not audio, so this is how podcast clips get shared. No watermark, and nothing is uploaded.

Ideas

  • Podcast episode teasers for Instagram, LinkedIn and X.
  • Voice notes and quotes from an interview.
  • Music previews with the cover art.
  • Course or webinar highlights for social posts.

Want the words on screen too?

Make the audiogram first, then run it through Auto captions to add captions that show as the person speaks. Most people watch social video with the sound off.

Frequently asked questions

Is my audio uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

How long can a clip be?

Up to 90 seconds. 30 to 60 seconds works best on social media and takes a minute or two to make.

Can I use a video file?

Yes. The sound is taken from the video.

Is there a watermark?

No.
