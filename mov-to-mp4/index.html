Frequently asked questions

Why convert MOV to MP4?

MP4 is widely accepted by browsers, social platforms, presentation software, and messaging apps.

Can this convert formats besides MOV?

Yes. The browser media engine can handle many common video containers, including WebM, MKV, and AVI, depending on their codecs.

Does the converter upload my video?

No. Conversion runs locally in your browser, although the media engine itself loads from this site.
