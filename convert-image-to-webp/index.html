Convert images to WebP

WebP files are usually 25–35% smaller than JPG or PNG at the same quality, so pages load faster. Convert one image or a whole folder’s worth at once, with transparency kept. Free and private.

When to use WebP

For images on your own website, online shop or blog. Every current browser shows WebP. For email attachments and printing, JPG is still the safer choice.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

Will transparency be kept?

Yes. Transparent PNGs stay transparent as WebP.

Can I convert back?

Yes. Pick JPG or PNG on the next screen.
