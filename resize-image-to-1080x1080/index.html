Resize a photo to 1080 × 1080

Make any photo exactly 1080 × 1080 px, the standard square for Instagram and Facebook. Crop to fill, or keep the whole photo with a border in any colour. Several photos at once, free and private.

Crop or border?

Crop to fill trims the edges evenly so the square is filled. Fit inside with a border keeps the whole photo, which suits products and artwork. You can switch on the next screen.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

Why 1080 × 1080?

It’s the size Instagram shows square posts at, and it works well on Facebook, LinkedIn and X too.

Can I do a batch?

Yes. Pick several photos and download them in one ZIP.
