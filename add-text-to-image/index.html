Add text to a photo

Type your words, pick where they go and how they look, and download the photo at full size. Clean captions, bold poster text, a dark box behind the words, or classic meme style. Free, no watermark, and your photo never leaves your device.

Ideas

  • Listings: “SOLD”, “NEW”, “SALE” or a price on product photos.
  • Social posts: a title or quote over a picture.
  • Event flyers: date and place over a photo.
  • Memes: white capitals with a black outline.

Tips

Short text reads best: three to six words. White with an outline or on a dark box stays readable on busy photos. For several lines, press Enter in the text box.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

Is there a watermark?

No. The download is your photo with only your text added.

Does it lower the quality?

No. The photo is saved at its full size; the preview you see is just smaller.
