Make an iPhone ringtone

Drop an MP3, M4A, voice memo or video, choose where it starts and how long it lasts (up to 40 seconds), and download an .m4r file, the format iPhones use for ringtones. No app to install, and your file stays on your device.

Ideas

  • The chorus of a song you own.
  • A voice memo as a contact’s ringtone.
  • A short sound as a text tone (15 seconds or less).
  • Audio from a video you filmed.

How to add it to your iPhone

On a Mac, connect the iPhone, open it in Finder and drag the .m4r onto it. On Windows, open iTunes or the Apple Devices app and drag it to Tones. Then choose it in Settings → Sounds & Haptics → Ringtone. You can also give one contact their own ringtone in the Contacts app.

Frequently asked questions

Why can’t I just download it on the iPhone?

iPhones only accept ringtones through a computer (Finder or iTunes) or GarageBand. Downloading the .m4r in Safari saves it to Files but doesn’t add it as a ringtone.

How long can it be?

Up to 40 seconds. 30 seconds is the usual length.

Is my file uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser.

Can I use Apple Music or Spotify songs?

No. Streaming songs aren’t files on your device. Use songs you own as files, or your own recordings.
