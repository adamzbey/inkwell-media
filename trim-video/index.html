Trim a video without uploading it

Free, private, in-browser video trimming — nothing leaves your device. Play the video, tap “Start here” and “End here”, and download just that part. Exact cuts land on the frames you picked; fast cuts skip re-encoding.

How trimming works here

Set a start and end point, and the tool exports just that segment, discarding the rest, using in-browser ffmpeg. It all runs on your device, so your video is never uploaded. Trimming to only the part you need is also the quickest way to make a video smaller.

When to trim a video

  • Cut dead air: remove slow starts and endings.
  • Grab a highlight: pull one moment out of a long recording.
  • Fit a limit: shorten a clip to get under an upload size cap.
  • Fix mistakes: cut out a fumble before sharing.

Trim first, then compress or convert

If a video is too big, trimming is the single biggest size saving you can make — a shorter clip is a smaller file, before you change anything else. Do your trimming first, then run the result through the compressor or converter if you still need to shrink or reformat it. Trimming is also non-destructive to your source: you're exporting a new clip, so the full-length original on your device stays exactly as it was.

Frequently asked questions

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Trimming runs entirely in your browser, so your video never leaves your device.

Does trimming re-encode the video?

Exact cut (the default) re-encodes at high quality so the clip starts and ends on the frames you chose. Fast cut copies the video without re-encoding, which is quicker but snaps to the nearest keyframe, so it can start a moment early.

How long or large can the video be?

The maximum is 350 MB per video; short clips under about 100 MB work best. Available browser memory may limit processing below the maximum, especially for high-resolution video. A computer is recommended.

Which formats are supported?

Common formats like MP4, MOV, and WebM.

Can I trim more than once?

Yes — export a segment, then trim again if you need to refine it.
