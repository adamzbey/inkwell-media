Speed up or slow down a video

Make a slow-motion clip, speed up a tutorial, or turn a long recording into a time-lapse. The sound is sped up or slowed down without the chipmunk effect. Free, private and in your browser.

How it works

The video is re-timed and re-encoded as an MP4. The sound is stretched or squeezed while keeping its pitch, so voices still sound natural. At 4× the sound is removed, as it would be unintelligible.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

Will slow motion look smooth?

It repeats frames, so a clip shot at 60 fps or more slows down best. Normal 30 fps clips look a little steppy at 0.5×.
