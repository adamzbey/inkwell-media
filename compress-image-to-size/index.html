Compress a photo to an exact size limit

Upload form says “max 200 KB”? Pick the limit and we get each photo just under it, keeping as much quality and size as possible. Free, private and in your browser, with several photos at once.

How it works

We try the highest quality that fits first, keeping the full picture size. Only if even low quality is too big do we make the picture smaller, step by step, until it fits. The result is saved as a JPG, the format upload forms expect (or WebP if you need to keep transparency).

Common limits

  • 50–100 KB: exam and job application forms, signatures, ID uploads.
  • 200 KB: many government and school portals.
  • 500 KB–1 MB: listing sites and support tickets.
  • 2 MB: most email and website contact forms.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

Will my photo still look good?

Usually yes down to about 100 KB for a typical photo. Below that, very detailed photos may look soft. We tell you if we had to shrink the picture.

Can I do several at once?

Yes. Pick several photos and you get them all under the limit in one ZIP.

Does it work with iPhone photos?

Yes. HEIC photos are converted to JPG as part of the process.
