Make a ringtone from any song

Drop a song or a video, play it and choose where the ringtone starts, then pick 15, 20, 30 or 40 seconds. It fades in and out and is turned up so you can hear it. Download an iPhone ringtone (.m4r) or an MP3 for Android. Everything happens on your device.

Ideas

  • A favourite song’s chorus as your ringtone.
  • A voice memo from your child or a friend as their contact’s tone.
  • A clip from a video you filmed.
  • An alert or text tone 15 seconds or less.

Putting it on an iPhone

iPhones only take ringtones through a computer or GarageBand. On a Mac, connect the iPhone, open it in Finder and drag the .m4r onto it. On Windows, use iTunes or the Apple Devices app and drag it to Tones. Then choose it in Settings → Sounds & Haptics → Ringtone.

Frequently asked questions

Is my song uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

How long can a ringtone be?

iPhone ringtones can be up to 40 seconds; 30 is the usual length. Text and alert tones work best under 15 seconds.

Does it work with Android?

Yes. Choose Android (.mp3), then copy the file to the phone’s Ringtones folder or open it on the phone and set it as the ringtone.

Can I use a song I bought?

Yes, if it isn’t copy-protected. Songs from streaming apps can’t be used because they aren’t files on your device.
