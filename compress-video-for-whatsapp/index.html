Compress a video for WhatsApp

Video too big to send, or taking forever? Get it under 16 MB, WhatsApp’s long-standing video limit, so it sends fast on any connection. We work out the best quality that fits. Free, and the video stays on your device.

How it works

We read how long the clip is, choose the quality and size that fit under 16 MB, and try once more if it comes out a little big. Longer clips are made smaller in size so they still look decent.

Sending the full-quality original instead

WhatsApp re-compresses videos you send as media. To send the original untouched, attach it as a Document instead (WhatsApp allows much larger documents).

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

Why 16 MB?

It’s the video limit WhatsApp used for years, and still a safe size: it sends quickly everywhere and works on older app versions and slow connections.

My video is long. Will it still fit?

A few minutes, yes, at lower resolution. For longer videos, trim them first.
