Make your screenshots look good

Paste or drop a screenshot and it sits on a clean gradient with a browser window or phone frame, rounded corners and a soft shadow. Pick a size for an X or LinkedIn post, Instagram, or just fit the screenshot. You see a live preview as you change it, and the picture never leaves your device.

Ideas

  • Product updates for X, LinkedIn and your changelog.
  • Slides and docs that look finished, not cropped from a screen.
  • App store and landing page pictures in a phone frame.
  • Support replies where a tidy screenshot explains it faster.

Tip: paste straight from the clipboard

Take a screenshot (Windows: Win+Shift+S, Mac: Cmd+Shift+4, then Ctrl or Cmd held while you click to copy it), come to this page and press Ctrl+V or Cmd+V. No saving needed.

Frequently asked questions

Are my screenshots uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your screenshots never leave your device.

Is there a watermark?

No. The picture is yours, with no watermark and no sign-up.

What size is the result?

“Fit the screenshot” keeps your screenshot at full size and adds the background around it. The post sizes (1600 × 900, 1200 × 627, 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350) fit it inside that shape.

Can I paste instead of choosing a file?

Yes. Copy a screenshot, then press Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on a Mac) on this page.
