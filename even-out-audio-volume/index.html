Even out the volume of a recording

Too quiet, or loud in some places and quiet in others? We bring the whole file to a steady standard level used by podcasts and video sites. Works on video (the picture is left as it is) and audio files.

How it works

We measure loudness the way broadcasters do (LUFS) and adjust the whole file to a target: −16 LUFS for podcasts and online video, −14 for music apps, or −23 for TV. Peaks are kept below clipping.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

Does it remove background noise?

No. It evens out the volume. Noise gets louder along with quiet speech, so record in a quiet room if you can.

Is the video re-encoded?

No. The picture is copied as it is; only the sound changes.
