Get the colour palette from a picture

Drop a photo, logo or screenshot and get its 5 to 10 main colours with hex codes. Click a swatch to copy its code, copy them all as CSS, or download a palette card with the picture and the codes underneath. The picture stays on your device.

Ideas

  • Match your brand colours from a logo you only have as a picture.
  • Decorating pull colours from a room photo or fabric.
  • Design start a website or slide theme from a photo you love.
  • Social posts pick text colours that suit the photo.

How the colours are picked

The picture is shrunk, then its pixels are split into groups of similar colour and each group is averaged. Colours that look the same are merged, so you get distinct colours, in order of how much of the picture they cover (or lightest to darkest).

Frequently asked questions

Are my pictures uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your pictures never leave your device.

Can I get the exact colour of one pixel?

This tool finds the main colours. For one exact pixel, open the picture in an editor with a colour picker.

What formats do the codes come in?

Hex codes like #CCA24E, and a CSS block with one variable per colour.

Does it work with iPhone photos?

Yes, HEIC photos work too.
