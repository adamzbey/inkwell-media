Cut an audio file

Drop an MP3, an M4A, a WAV, a voice memo or even a video. Play it, choose where to start and end, add a fade if you like, and save just that part as MP3, M4A or WAV. Everything happens on your device.

Good for

  • A clip of a podcast or interview to share.
  • The useful part of a voice memo or a lecture recording.
  • An intro or outro for a video or podcast.
  • A song section for a slideshow or a dance routine.

Frequently asked questions

Is my audio uploaded?

No. It’s cut in your browser, so the file never leaves your device.

Which files work?

MP3, M4A, AAC, WAV, OGG, Opus, FLAC and most voice-memo formats, plus the sound from MP4 or MOV videos.

Will cutting lower the quality?

The part you keep is saved again as MP3 or M4A at the quality you pick (192 kbps is good for nearly everything), or as WAV with no loss at all.

Can I make a ringtone?

Yes, or use the Ringtone maker, which makes an iPhone .m4r or Android MP3 of the right length for you.
