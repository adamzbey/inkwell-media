Private browser tool

Copy text from an image

Drop a screenshot, a scanned page, a receipt or a photo of a document and get the words as text you can copy, edit or search. It reads English, Spanish, French and German. Everything happens on your device, so it’s safe for private documents.

Good for

  • A screenshot of a message, a slide or an error you need to paste somewhere.
  • A photo of a printed page or a handout you want to edit.
  • Receipts and invoices for your expense notes.
  • A sign or menu in another language, to paste into a translator.

Frequently asked questions

Is my image uploaded?

No. The text reader runs in your browser, so the picture never leaves your device. That makes it fine for bank letters, IDs and other private documents.

How accurate is it?

Very good on clear screenshots and scans. Photos taken at an angle, in poor light or with handwriting are harder. Always check names, numbers and amounts before you rely on them.

Which languages does it read?

English, Spanish, French and German, plus English and Spanish together for mixed text. The reader for each language downloads once (about 3 MB) and is then cached.

Can I do several pictures at once?

Yes. Choose several and you get a ZIP with one text file for each picture.
