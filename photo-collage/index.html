Make a photo collage or grid

Pick your photos, put them in order and download one image: square, Instagram portrait, story, wide or a 6 × 4 print. Each photo fills its space neatly; choose the spacing, the background colour and rounded corners. No watermark, and your photos stay on your device.

Ideas

  • Before and after side by side.
  • Product grids for a listing or a flyer.
  • Trip or event highlights for one Instagram post.
  • A printed 6 × 4 of several photos for the fridge.

How the layout works

Automatic picks a neat grid for the number of photos and the shape you chose; set “Photos per row” to choose it yourself. If the last row has fewer photos, they share the full width, so there are no empty gaps.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

How many photos can I use?

2 to 24.

Will my photos be squashed?

No. Each photo is trimmed evenly at the edges to fill its space, never stretched.

Does it work with iPhone photos?

Yes, HEIC photos work too.
