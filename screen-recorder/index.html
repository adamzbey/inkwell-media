Private browser tool

Record your screen, free and private

Record a tutorial, a bug report or a quick walkthrough. Choose your whole screen, one window or a browser tab, add your microphone and the tab’s sound, then download the video. Nothing to install, no watermark, and the recording never leaves your computer.

How to record

  • Click Start recording and choose what to share.
  • Do your thing. The timer shows how long you’ve been recording.
  • Click Stop recording (or your browser’s “Stop sharing”) and download it.

Tips

Close notifications and other tabs first. Recording a single tab gives the cleanest result and lets you include that tab’s sound. For slides and documents, 15 frames a second makes a much smaller file.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

Is there a time limit or watermark?

No and no. The only limit is your computer’s memory; an hour at 30 frames a second is fine on most laptops.

What format is the video?

MP4 in Chrome and Edge on recent versions, otherwise WebM. Use Convert to MP4 if you need MP4 from a WebM.

Does it work on phones?

No. Phone and tablet browsers don’t allow screen recording; use your phone’s built-in recorder.
