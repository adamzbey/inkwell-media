Compress a photo to under 100 KB

Need a photo under 100 KB for a form? Drop it in and download a copy that fits, with as much quality as 100 KB allows. Several photos at once, free and private.

How it works

We keep the picture at full size and lower the quality just enough to fit. If that isn’t enough, we shrink the picture step by step until it’s under the limit. You can change the limit on the next screen.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

Why do forms ask for 100 KB?

Many government, school and job portals cap uploads to keep their systems fast. A phone photo is often 2–5 MB, so it needs shrinking first.

Will it still be clear enough?

For ID photos and signatures, yes. We tell you if the picture had to be made smaller.
