Convert audio to MP3

Drop a WAV, M4A, OGG, Opus, FLAC or AAC file, or a voice memo, and save it as an MP3 that plays everywhere. You can also save as M4A or WAV, and trim the start and end at the same time. Everything happens on your device.

Good for

  • Voice memos from an iPhone (.m4a) for a computer or an email.
  • WAV recordings that are too big to send.
  • OGG or Opus files from apps that older players can’t open.
  • FLAC music for a car or phone that only plays MP3.

Frequently asked questions

Is my audio uploaded?

No. It’s converted in your browser, so the file never leaves your device.

Which quality should I pick?

Standard (192 kbps) sounds the same as the original for nearly everyone. Pick Best (320 kbps) for music you care about, or Smaller (128 kbps) for speech.

Can I convert a whole album at once?

One file at a time for now. Each conversion takes a few seconds for a song.

Can I get the sound out of a video?

Yes. Drop the video here, or use Extract audio.
