Make a profile picture

Drop a photo and your face is found and centred in a circle. Drag and zoom to adjust, add a coloured ring if you like, and download a sharp PNG with a transparent background, or a square version that works everywhere.

Sizes

  • 400 × 400: LinkedIn, Slack, Teams, Gmail and Outlook.
  • 800 × 800: a sharp all-rounder.
  • 1080 × 1080: Instagram, Facebook and TikTok.

Tips for a good profile photo

Face the light, keep the background simple, and fill most of the circle with your head and shoulders. A ring in your brand colour helps you stand out in a list of names.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

Why a transparent background?

Outside the circle stays see-through, so it looks right on light and dark pages. Choose White or Keep it square if a site doesn’t accept PNG.

Does the face finder upload my photo?

No. It’s a small model that runs in your browser.
